Training orientation

A self-paced orientation course for new team members, volunteers, community members, and partner organizations who are just beginning to engage with food sovereignty advocacy. After completing it, learners will be able to explain the core values of food sovereignty and identify practical ways to support a community-led food system.

Branching scenario

A decision-based scenario where learners practice a workplace conversation and see the results of their choices. 

The situation: You're a new advocate who has run into a challenge you're not sure how to handle. You've set up a conversation with your mentor, an experienced advocate, to talk it through. As you do, you'll decide how to describe the issue, how to use the resource toolkit, and how to ask for a peer's perspective.

Goal: Tests whether the learner raises an advocacy challenge and seeks peer perspective without treating resource use as inadequacy or minimizing the issue.
