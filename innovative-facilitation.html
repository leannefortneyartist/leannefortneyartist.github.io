Innovative Facilitation

Think differently. Create boldly. Solve meaningfully.

What if the key to solving your most complex challenges wasn't more data or more meetings — but a fundamentally different way of thinking? Drawing on the principles of creative practice, Leanne Fortney brings an artist's perspective to the world of problem-solving and innovation.

Through facilitated workshops designed to unlock creative thinking, teams and individuals learn to move beyond habitual patterns, challenge assumptions, and discover unexpected solutions. Whether you're facing organizational challenges, creative blocks, or the need for fresh perspectives, these sessions create space for genuine innovation to emerge.
